# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard 

This project presents a **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes employee attendance and work patterns.  
The goal of this HR Analytics project is to provide insights into workforce productivity, attendance consistency, and remote work behavior during the period **April to June 2022**.

---

## 🧾 Overview

The dashboard was built using **Power BI** and is powered by the dataset `Attendance-Sheet-2022-2023.xlsx`.  
It focuses on three major metrics:
- **Attendance Percentage (94%)**
- **Work From Home (WFH) Percentage (9.1%)**
- **Sick Leave (SL) Percentage (0.4%)**

Through these KPIs, HR managers can identify attendance trends, employee reliability, and overall workforce engagement.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

1. **Overall Attendance**
   - The average attendance rate across all employees is **94%**, showing a highly engaged and disciplined workforce.
   - Attendance remained stable between **92%–97%** throughout the analysis period.

2. **Work From Home (WFH) Patterns**
   - **9.1% of total workdays** were spent working from home.
   - **Friday (12.2%)** and **Thursday (10.3%)** had the highest WFH percentages — indicating employees prefer flexible work arrangements toward the end of the week.
   - WFH peaked at **16.4% in late April**, possibly due to hybrid work policies or project flexibility.

3. **Sick Leave (SL) Trends**
   - The overall Sick Leave rate is just **0.43%**, suggesting good employee health and low absenteeism.
   - **Thursday (1.3%)** saw the highest SL percentage, hinting at mid-week fatigue or personal leave trends.

4. **Day-of-Week Attendance**
   - Attendance is **highest on Mondays (96.5%) and Tuesdays (95.5%)**, indicating strong early-week productivity.
   - Attendance drops to **90.3% on Thursdays**, showing a mild pre-weekend dip that HR can address through engagement programs.

5. **Employee-Level Observations**
   - Top employees such as **Atq-403**, **Atq-335**, and **Atq-436** maintained attendance above **85%**.  
   - A few employees recorded attendance below **70%**, signaling potential performance or engagement issues.

6. **Time-Based Trends**
   - Attendance dipped slightly around **mid-April** but recovered towards month-end.  
   - Sick Leave and WFH trends remained consistent and well-balanced, ensuring overall productivity stability.

7. **HR Interpretation**
   - maintains a **healthy hybrid work culture** with strong accountability.  
   - The combination of **high attendance, low sick leave, and consistent WFH behavior** reflects positive morale and flexible workforce management.
   - Focused engagement on Thursdays could further optimize attendance rates.
